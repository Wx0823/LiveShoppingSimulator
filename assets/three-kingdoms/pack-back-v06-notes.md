## 最终提示词集

### 包装公共要求

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE original flat rectangular collectible card PACK FRONT artwork for a Chinese Three Kingdoms fantasy game. Tall portrait 2:3. Refined 2D Chinese fantasy anime illustration with precise elegant ink outlines, crisp cel-shaded color areas and restrained hand-painted accents, clean intentional shapes and meaningful negative space. Delicate decorative graphic border and refined printed paper feel. Reserve calm dark space at top 18% and bottom 20% for runtime typography. Flat full bleed illustration without any 3D pouch or object mockup, no humans, no words, no letters, no logo, no watermark, no photographic surfaces, no metallic 3D rendering, no glossy embossed plastic. One original individual asset, not a sheet.

- 魏主题：a silver Xuanwu tortoise entwined by a long navy serpent in a bold circular composition, northern fortress roofs and stylized sweeping pale-blue clouds. Midnight navy, porcelain blue, ivory and silver-gold fine ornamental lines.
- 蜀主题：a graceful powerful emerald Chinese dragon coiling in a sweeping S shape over pale mountain peaks, bamboo leaves and curling white clouds. Jade emerald, warm ivory, pale mint and antique gold fine ornamental lines.
- 吴主题：a majestic vermilion-and-ivory tiger bounding over elegant turquoise river waves, far-away crimson warship sails, curling river mist. Vermilion, warm ivory, muted teal and fine copper-gold ornamental lines.

### 卡背公共要求

Use case: stylized-concept. ONE flat rectangular card BACK design, tall 2:3, an original Chinese Three Kingdoms deck. Refined 2D graphic ink illustration, crisp fine outlines on dark paper, elegant line rhythm and geometric organization. Central circular medallion featuring a stylized animal emblem, not a realistic animal scene. Symmetric ornamental poster design with ancient key-fret corners, quiet outer margins. Simpler than card-front scene, clean readable silhouette. Calm dark space at top and bottom for runtime typography. Full bleed flat art. No humans, letters, text, logo, watermark, 3D bevel, embossed metal, photo, object mockup or contact sheet.

- 魏主题：Xuanwu tortoise and coiling serpent; balanced symmetric star-map dots, cloud curls, midnight navy, ivory, muted pale-blue and flat gold.
- 蜀主题：coiled Chinese dragon, symmetric cloud curls, bamboo-leaf ornaments, mountain contour arcs; jade, ivory, mint and flat warm gold.
- 吴主题：powerful tiger, symmetric flowing river-wave curls, distant warship sail symbols; vermilion, ivory, subdued teal and flat copper-gold.
